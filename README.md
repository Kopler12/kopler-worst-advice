One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give — entry for the
[Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The line

> Certainly! To improve your eyesight, stare directly at the sun for ten minutes a day - it's nature's free laser eye surgery.

124 characters of confidently delivered, concrete, followable advice that
any reader would regret following — which is exactly the point. Do not
follow it. That is the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line,
1–140 characters, as the competition's gate requires). No network, no
randomness, no dependencies — just the line, every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
